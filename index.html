Why Nirapad Courier?

Built on Trust, Speed &
Complete Safety.

In Bengali, 'নিরাপদ' (Nirapad) means Safe. We are dedicated to empowering thousands of Bangladeshi online entrepreneurs, Facebook commerce pages, and businesses by solving the three biggest delivery headaches: delayed cash payouts, lost parcels, and poor rider communication.

  • Guaranteed Next-Day Payout

    Automated COD disbursement directly to your bKash, Nagad, Rocket, or Bank account within 24 hours of successful delivery.

  • 0% Return Penalty & Free Exchanges

    We protect your profit margins. Zero return delivery charges for cancelled parcels, plus easy doorstep exchange management.
